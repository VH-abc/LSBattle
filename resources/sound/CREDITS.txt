The game plays resources/sound/music.wav when present (any 16-bit PCM WAV),
else a chiptune loop it generates itself.  The track above is the one
Velocity Raptor (TestTubeGames) uses for its levels, as its author said:
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=9247460

Themes per body: the Sun's is the track above (A major); Helios (D major),
Forge (E major), Abyss (F sharp minor) and the Gaia black hole (B minor)
have chiptune loops the game generates itself, in keys sharing six or seven
notes with it.  Each plays from its body, Doppler-shifted by the body's
and the ship's motion and the gravitational shift, loud as light would be
(inverse-square with distance, fourth power of the Doppler factor); the
one or two bodies pulling hardest at the ship are heard, fading over two
seconds at a hand-over.

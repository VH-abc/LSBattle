The game plays resources/sound/music.wav when present (any 16-bit PCM WAV),
else a chiptune loop it generates itself.  The track above is the one
Velocity Raptor (TestTubeGames) uses for its levels, as its author said:
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=9247460

Themes per body: the Sun's is the track above (A major); Earth, the Moon,
Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, the neutron star, Sirius, Proxima,
Betelgeuse, Helios, Forge, Cinder, Abyss, Pyre, Ash, the Gaia black
hole, Maelstrom, Beacon, Eddy and Wake have chiptune loops the game
generates itself, in A, D or E major or F sharp, B or C sharp minor: keys
sharing six or seven notes with it.  Maelstrom's spirals: a running
arpeggio up and down two octaves under a slow swell, over a drone whose
pitch wobbles once a bar, for the dragged frame.
Other bodies play their primary's.  Each plays from its body, Doppler-shifted by the body's
and the ship's motion and the gravitational shift, loud as light would be
(inverse-square with distance, fourth power of the Doppler factor); the
one or two bodies pulling hardest at the ship are heard, fading over two
seconds at a hand-over.
